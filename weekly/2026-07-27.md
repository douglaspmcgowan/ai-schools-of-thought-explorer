## Techniques & tricks

**Explicit output-format specification** ([getprompting.com/prompt-engineering-guide](https://getprompting.com/prompt-engineering-guide/)): Specifying the exact output structure (JSON, markdown table, numbered list, code block) in your prompt reduces downstream post-processing by 60–80% in production workflows.

**"Think step by step" suffix on complex prompts** ([getprompting.com/prompt-engineering-guide](https://getprompting.com/prompt-engineering-guide/)): Appending "think step by step" or "reason through this before answering" to multi-step logic tasks improves LLM accuracy by ~40% with zero extra tooling.
